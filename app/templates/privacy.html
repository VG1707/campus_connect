Purpose Limitation

Timetable information is solely used to find non-conflicting consultation slots with faculty. It is never sold, shared, or used for grading or student profiling.

🗑️

Immediate Image Deletion

Screenshot uploads are temporary files. The moment you confirm or cancel your extracted timetable grid, the image is permanently wiped from disk. Zero image bytes stored.

🛡️

Data Minimization

We never ask for college portal passwords, government IDs (Aadhaar), phone numbers, or academic marks. We store only period numbers and course codes.

1. Data Collected & Storage Scope

  • User Profile: Name, institutional email, simulated role (Student/Faculty/Admin), and Department.
  • Timetable Records: Day order / weekday, period number (1 through 8), room number, and course code.
  • Meeting Inquiries: Topic tags (e.g. "Pointers in C"), preferred time window, booking status, and counter-proposal notes.
  • Extraction Benchmark Logs: Anonymous counts of extracted cells and review time in seconds. No personal identifiers attached.

2. Retention and Expiration

Student timetable mappings automatically expire at the conclusion of each academic semester. Consultation records and audit logs are retained during the active term for academic integrity and conflict verification, after which they are purged.

3. Role-Based Privacy & Confidentiality

Students can never view another student's full timetable, attendance, or private doubt descriptions. When group sessions are suggested, faculty see aggregated counts and enrolled names, but individual student notes remain private.
